matlab-ascii-art Image → ASCII art in base MATLAB

        MATLAB · Vectorization · Unit Testing · Image Processing
      

      
    
        
  • Grayscale or per-character color output, Unicode charsets (░▒▓█), tone controls, export to text,
          PNG or HTML, and an interactive app with live preview that uploads images even in MATLAB Online
